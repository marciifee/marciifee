### 🌸 Aloha! I'm Marc 👋  

My name is **Marc Langenberg** — I’m an **IT specialist** with a focus on **cybersecurity** and **cloud solutions**, and the creative mind behind my passion project **CosplayGIF**.  
I’m currently preparing for my exam to become an **Certified Information Security Specialist (CISS) (CCI) / geprüfter Berufsspezialist in Informationssicherheit (IHK)**, deepening my expertise in secure system architecture and compliance.

### 🎭 About Me  

For more than **ten years**, I’ve been deeply involved in the **cosplay and convention community**, working **voluntarily as a division and team leader at DoKomi Expo in Düsseldorf, Germany** from **2014 to 2024**.  
The unique energy, creativity, and passion of the cosplay scene have fascinated me ever since and continue to influence both my **technical** and **creative** projects today.  

With over a **decade of experience as a photographer and retoucher** (for brands such as *Ulla Popken, Douglas,* and *dm*), I combine **creative vision** with **technical expertise**.  
My journey from **digital film production** to **commercial fashion photography** and **IT** taught me how to merge creativity and technology in **secure, smart, and innovative ways**.
